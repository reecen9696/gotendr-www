Takeoffs and BOQs

Quantity takeoffs and bills of quantities

Measuring scope off the drawings, structuring a BOQ, and the errors that cost the most.

  • What are the most common landscape takeoff mistakes?

    The most common landscape takeoff mistakes are measuring at the wrong scale, pricing a superseded revision, missing the specification, and miscounting plants against the schedule.

    23 September 2026

  • How do you calculate topsoil, mulch and turf quantities for a landscape job?

    Measure the area of each bed or lawn from the drawings, then multiply area in square metres by the specified depth in metres to get volume in cubic metres.

    23 September 2026

  • How do you do a landscape quantity takeoff from drawings?

    You do a landscape quantity takeoff by collecting the full drawing set, checking scale and revision, then measuring every scope item by trade as an area, length, count or volume.

    23 September 2026

  • What is a bill of quantities in landscaping?

    A bill of quantities (BOQ) in landscaping is the itemised schedule of every element in a landscape scope, with a description, unit and measured quantity on each line.

    23 September 2026
